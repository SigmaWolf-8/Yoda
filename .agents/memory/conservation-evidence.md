---
name: Conservation evidence boundaries
description: Why accounting residuals and supplied bridge scenarios must not become physical or legal attestations.
---

Accounting reconciliation is a discrete model, not a proved identification of
money with a differentiable physical vector field. A zero valuation gap is not
physical losslessness or zero entropy; finite reserve checks are not continuous
backing or statutory compliance.

**Why:** the supplied divergence-flux mapping conflates these claims and
contradicts itself about the XRP price: 200000 NMT at 0.12 USD/NMT gives a cost
basis of 24000 USD; an XRP price of 23999.50 gives a 0.50 gap, whereas a 0.50
price gives a 23999.50 gap. Comparing USD/XRP with USD/NMT is not valid.

**How to apply:** compute from explicit inputs, keep units and observation
periods visible, retain failures per observation even when residuals cancel,
and identify source-provided scenarios as scenarios, not executed settlements.
Do not invent missing bank, meter, token, or journal event histories. Supplemental
audits must not silently rewrite historical sealed books or issue transactions.
Hash-chain integrity does not establish the truth of evidence or an external signature.

For Earth reconstruction, symbolic identities transform declared observations;
they do not generate missing terrain, geoid measurements, datum transformations
or uncertainty estimates. Preserve supplied instruments as reference artifacts
and put accessibility/integration changes in explicitly identified derivatives.

**Why:** the supplied Earth specification separates a coarse cartographic
demonstrator from a proposed reconstruction system. Calling the supplied package
“full” is not permission to invent the proposed system's absent observations or
claim survey-grade accuracy.

**How to apply:** expose the full supplied functionality while identifying
dataset and datum limitations. Keep server-side canonical assertions, browser
instrument assertions, finite numerical checks and actual daemon executions
distinct; success in one does not certify the others.

Private browser evidence history is an explicit exception to the open,
single-operator application access policy, not permission to reinstate a global
login gate.

**Why:** persistent reports require account isolation, but storing a browser's
claims on the server cannot turn them into authenticated daemon receipts. The
browser controls its inputs, version strings, timestamps and results.

**How to apply:** preserve the distinction between authenticated submitter,
server receipt time, and claimed execution provenance. Do not upgrade trust
because a report survived reload or carries an ID, timestamp or digest.
