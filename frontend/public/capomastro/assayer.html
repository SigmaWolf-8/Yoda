I.1 The GENIUS Act (Public Law 119-27; 12 U.S.C. §§ 5901–5916)

  • Enactment: 18 July 2025.
  • Effective Date: The earlier of 18 January 2027 or 120 days after final implementing regulations.
  • Scope: Federal statutory framework governing the issuance, reserves, redemption, disclosure, supervision, and enforcement of payment stablecoins in the United States.

A payment stablecoin is a digital asset designed for payment or settlement that the issuer is obligated to redeem or repurchase at a fixed monetary value (ordinarily one U.S. dollar). Only a Permitted Payment Stablecoin Issuer (PPSI) may lawfully issue such instruments after the effective date.

  • Exclusions: Tokenized bank deposits and pure commodity or energy tokens that exist solely on private infrastructure and lack a fixed fiat redemption promise fall outside the GENIUS Act definition and therefore outside its reserve mandates.
