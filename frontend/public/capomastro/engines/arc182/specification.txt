## 0. Corrections incorporated (binding)

These four corrections supersede the prior characterization and are normative for this spec:

| # | Prior statement (superseded) | Corrected statement (binding) |
|---|---|---|
| C-1 | "Zero runtime dependencies is a property of the visual tier only" | **Zero runtime dependencies is achievable for both tiers** by baking all data (WGS84 parameters, Natural Earth, DEM grid, geoid grid) into the artifact. The dependency is relocated from runtime to build/provenance. |
| C-2 | "Pure first-principles math only gives synthetic terrain" | **Correct.** Pure math produces the smooth ellipsoid plus fractal/synthetic surfaces. It is *not* a substitute for Earth. |
| C-3 | "You would need to invent geology, tectonics, erosion…" | **Not required and not attempted.** The engine consumes *observed* heights (satellite radar, lidar, photogrammetry, surveyed benchmarks) via public DEMs and geoid models. No synthetic geology is generated. |
| C-4 | "Math cannot produce a real DEM" | **Correct in the strict sense**, but the math layer *does* interpolate, compress, encode, resample, and hillshade observed DEMs. The engine is a transformation/encoding layer over public observations, not an observation source. |

**Separation of concerns (normative):**

- **Data layer** = observed (DEM, geoid, authoritative coastlines, CRS parameters)
- **Math layer** = interpolation, compression, encoding, transformation, rendering
- **Software layer** = deterministic, self-verifying, zero-runtime-dependency

The engine is only as accurate as the data layer. The math layer cannot manufacture observations; it can only transport, transform, and render them faithfully.

## 1. Purpose & scope

### 1.1 Purpose
Define a best-in-class, standards-traceable specification for a browser-native Earth model that simultaneously supports:

- **Tier 1 — Visual/Cartographic Globe**: WGS84 ellipsoid + Natural Earth basemap + coarse ETOPO relief. Zero runtime dependencies. Legitimate.
- **Tier 2 — Geodetic / Terrain Reconstruction**: Tier 1 plus a geoid model and a high-resolution DEM. Zero runtime dependencies (baked in) but with declared accuracy and provenance. Legitimate *as an interpolation and rendering engine over public observations*, not as an observation source.

### 1.2 In scope
- Reference ellipsoid and CRS handling
- Basemap, terrain, and (for Tier 2) geoid layers
- Coordinate transformations (geodetic ↔ ECEF ↔ screen)
- Projections (3D globe; north-polar azimuthal equidistant)
- Relief hillshading and biome tinting
- Runtime self-assertion and rejection
- Data provenance, hashing, licensing
- Zero-runtime-dependency packaging
- Accuracy statements and uncertainty declarations
- Accessibility and security posture

### 1.3 Out of scope
- Primary observation (the engine does not survey)
- Cadastral, legal-boundary, or navigation-certified use
- Real-time Earth orientation (ITRF epoch handling beyond WGS84 static)
- Legal metrology certification

## 2. Terminology

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **WGS84** | World Geodetic System 1984. Reference ellipsoid + datum. Parameters per NGA.STND.0036. |
| **Ellipsoid height h** | Height above the WGS84 ellipsoid. |
| **Orthometric height H** | Height above the geoid (mean sea level). |
| **Geoid undulation N** | N = h − H. The geoid–ellipsoid separation. |
| **DEM** | Digital Elevation Model. A gridded field of heights. |
| **Geoid model** | A gridded or spherical-harmonic field of N. |
| **Tier 1** | Visual/cartographic globe. |
| **Tier 2** | Geodetic/terrain reconstruction tier. |
| **Zero-runtime-dependency** | The shipped artifact requires no network fetch, no external library, and no CDN at execution time. All inputs are baked in. |
| **Provenance** | The chain of custody of data: source, version, hash, license. |

## 4. Epistemic positioning (claims and non-claims)

The engine makes **explicit, falsifiable** claims and refuses to make others.

### 4.1 Claims (Tier 1)
- The rendered solid is a WGS84 ellipsoid to the declared parameter precision.
- The overlay is Natural Earth 110m (generalized, cartographic).
- The relief field is ETOPO 1-degree (coarse, ~111 km cells at the equator; ~100 m vertical RMSE class).
- Coastlines are not navigation-grade.
- Heights are *ellipsoidal*, not orthometric. (Tier 1 has no geoid model.)
- All numbers on the page are recomputed by BigInt at open time from the pipe-glyph registry; no pre-baked figures.

### 4.2 Claims (Tier 2)
- Heights are reported in **both** ellipsoidal (h) and orthometric (H = h − N) form.
- The geoid model is declared (EGM2008 / XGM2019e / national) with version and grid resolution.
- The DEM is declared (Copernicus GLO-30 / SRTM / NASADEM) with version, resolution, vertical datum, and accuracy statement.
- Coastlines are declared (GSHHG version / national hydrographic version).
- Accuracy is declared per layer, not aggregated into a single falsely precise number.

### 4.3 Non-claims (both tiers)
- The engine is **not** a survey instrument.
- The engine does **not** produce real Earth observations; it interpolates, compresses, encodes, and renders public observations.
- The engine does **not** invent geology, tectonics, erosion, sedimentation, or impacts.
- The engine is **not** legal-for-trade, cadastral, or navigation-certified.

### 4.4 Explicit refusal of the "mannequin-and-costume" framing
The prior framing ("smooth mannequin wearing a costume") is **partially rejected**: it correctly distinguishes basemap from ellipsoid, but it **understates** the tool because ETOPO relief and hillshading are already present in v0.0.20. The engine is better described as:

> *A WGS84 ellipsoid with a public-domain basemap and a coarse public DEM, rendered with physical hillshading and biome tinting, under zero-runtime-dependency, self-verifying arithmetic.*

That is stronger than "costume" and weaker than "reconstruction." The tiering in §3 makes the exact position precise.

## 7. Mathematical architecture

### 7.1 Reference ellipsoid
- WGS84 ellipsoid, flattening applied via `b/a` in every surface intersection.
- No sphere shortcut is used; the flattening appears in the ray–ellipsoid inverse.

### 7.2 Geodetic ↔ ECEF
- Implemented in the projection layer (`projectXYZ`, `projectEarth`).
- Tier 2 must add the inverse ECEF → geodetic (h, φ, λ) using Bowring's method or a convergent iteration to declared tolerance.

### 7.3 Projections
- **3D globe**: perspective from an eye point; per-pixel ray–ellipsoid intersection.
- **North-polar azimuthal equidistant**: ρ = (90° − φ) · Rmax/180°; true distance from the pole; Tissot indicatrices expose the distortion.

### 7.4 Interpolation and resampling
- Bilinear interpolation for elevation (`elevAt`).
- Tier 2: bicubic or spline for high-res DEM; explicit declaration of interpolation kernel and its effect on smoothness/accuracy.

### 7.5 Hillshading
- NW illumination via gradient of the elevation field.
- Tier 2: declare the illumination azimuth, elevation, and z-factor.

### 7.6 Climate and biome tinting
- Analytic aridity function (subtropical belts + named dry cores).
- Latitude-dependent snowline.
- Declared as **visualization**, not classification.

### 7.7 Zero-float arithmetic layer (isopsephy)
- BigInt arithmetic in the isopsephy engine; no IEEE-754 in the arithmetic layer.
- This is a deliberate two-layer policy: geometry uses IEEE-754 (with declared precision), isopsephy uses BigInt (exact).
- The two layers are separated by the data model; no isopsephy number crosses into the geometry layer as a float.

## 8. Runtime architecture

### 8.1 Zero-runtime-dependency posture

| Property | Status |
|---|---|
| External HTTP fetches at open time | 0 |
| External CDN | 0 |
| External libraries | 0 |
| Baked data | WGS84 parameters, Natural Earth, ETOPO, biome model |
| Baked (Tier 2) | Geoid model, high-res DEM, GSHHG or authoritative coastline |
| Browser requirement | HTML5 + Canvas 2D + BigInt |
| No network required after first byte | yes |

**Definitional note:** Zero *runtime* dependencies is a packaging property. The data still has upstream sources; those are declared in the provenance manifest. The engine relocates the dependency from runtime to build.

### 8.2 Rendering
- Canvas 2D.
- Persistent offscreen buffer for the relief shader (no per-frame reallocation).
- Painter's algorithm for word points.
- Target: full-resolution (q = 2 supersampling) when the camera changes; cached otherwise.

### 8.3 Self-assertion layer
- §0 axioms in the HTML: BigInt recomputation at open time, rejection-on-failure.
- §9 fold certificate: live checks of the framework identities.
- Tier 2: extend the assertion layer to check geoid/DEM block hashes and declared bounds.

### 8.4 State management
- Explicit camera state, pattern lens, and zoom.
- No hidden global mutation outside the documented state object.

## 15. Verdict and recommended statement

### 15.1 Direct answer to the original question, corrected

> **Tier 1 — Visual/Cartographic Globe (v0.0.20 as shipped): YES, legitimate.**
> It is a WGS84 ellipsoid with the flattening honored in the math, a public-domain Natural Earth basemap, a baked ETOPO 1-degree relief field with hillshading and biome tinting, rendered at zero runtime dependencies, with live self-assertion. It is *not* a survey instrument, and *does not claim to be.*

> **Tier 2 — Geodetic / Terrain Reconstruction: NOT YET, and achievable with additive layers.**
> Add a geoid model (EGM2008 or XGM2019e), a high-resolution DEM (Copernicus GLO-30, SRTM v3, or NASADEM), an authoritative coastline (GSHHG or national hydrographic), a vertical datum declaration (EPSG:3855), and a per-layer uncertainty statement. All of these are public-domain or open-license; all can be baked in; zero runtime dependencies is preserved.

> **Pure-math-only, in the strict sense: NO.** Pure first-principles math yields the smooth ellipsoid and synthetic surfaces. It cannot yield Earth. The math layer's role is to interpolate, compress, encode, and render *observed* public data; it is not the observation source.

### 15.2 Recommended public statement (accurate and falsifiable)

> *Arc182 Earth Engine, v0.0.20 — a WGS84-ellipsoid, public-domain basemap globe with baked ETOPO 1-degree relief, hillshaded, biome-tinted, rendered at zero runtime dependencies, with live BigInt self-assertion. Tier 1 (visual/cartographic) is certified for visualization and education. Tier 2 (geodetic/terrain reconstruction) is planned as an additive layer set: geoid model, 30 m DEM, authoritative coastline, per-layer uncertainty. The engine is an interpolation and rendering layer over public observations; it is not an observation source and is not a survey instrument.*

That statement is defensible to a geodetic reviewer, a cartographic reviewer, and a software-engineering reviewer simultaneously. It holds all four corrections (C-1 through C-4) and covers the full standards matrix in §5.
